PLAY LITTLE DRONE

Open this link on your phone:

https://boxyroom.github.io/4.1-Final-JS-Project/little-drone/

1. Open the link in Chrome or Safari
2. Turn your phone sideways (landscape)
3. Tap Play
4. Optional: Add to Home Screen / Install app for a full-screen icon

Controls
- Left stick: move
- Orange ↑ button: lift up (release to sink)
- Boost: speed burst (uses battery)
- Radar: reveal nearby gems
- Scan: find hidden supply crates
- Desktop: WASD + Space lift + Shift boost

Goal: fly through all 12 glowing rings before your battery hits zero.
